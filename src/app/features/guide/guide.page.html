The idea in one minute

  1. Buy the booklet at a designated counter (see below). It is a souvenir journal, not a travel document.
  2. Go to the post office listed for a page. The pictorial cancellation is kept at that office, which is not always at the monument itself.
  3. Buy a postage stamp, stick it on the page, and ask the counter to cancel it with the Permanent Pictorial Cancellation (PPC).
  4. Tick it here, add the date and a photo of the ink impression, and watch your district map fill up.

Stock and counters change. Check the circle's social media or call the head post office before travelling.

Tips from collectors

  • Check the office, not the monument. Several cancellations are held at a town office some distance from the site.
  • Branch offices (BO) keep short hours, often a few hours in the morning, and close on Sundays and holidays. Go early on weekdays.
  • Carry small change and a few spare stamps. Some counters run out of low-value stamps.
  • Ask politely for a clean, full impression. Hold the page flat and let the ink dry before closing the booklet.
  • Cluster your trips. Use Near me → Weekend clusters to group nearby offices, and stay in the nearest larger town.
  • Back up your ticks from the Progress page. Data lives only in this browser.

  • Karnataka: {{ stats().ka }} of 100 locations, each matched to a known PPC. The list comes from an unofficial tracker, not the printed booklet. {{ stats().conflicts }} entries have a PIN that differs between sources and are flagged.
  • Tamil Nadu: {{ stats().tn }} pictorial cancellations in the Tamil Nadu circle (incl. Puducherry) for 58 booklet slots. Press says the passport gathers the circle's existing PPCs, but the official 58-list is not online; {{ stats().tnPress }} are named in press coverage.
  • Coordinates: {{ stats().osm }} matched to an OpenStreetMap post office; {{ stats().town }} are town or village centres (OSM Nominatim); {{ stats().geonames }} are GeoNames localities; {{ stats().centroid }} are PIN-area centroids. Everything except OSM post-office matches is approximate and shown with a dashed ring.
  • Guide text is short original wording written for this app (marked "editorial"). It is not copied from the booklet.
  • Post office hours are not included yet because no reliable public source was found.
